1. Operational Use of the Platform

AgroPulse provides environmental telemetry monitoring, Random Forest classification decision support, crop plot registration, and irrigation relay control for authorized farm operators. By signing in, you confirm that you are authorized to monitor and actuate irrigation equipment for the registered farm sectors.

2. Field Equipment Actuation & Safety Responsibility

Manual and automated pump commands affect physical water distribution and solenoid valves. Operators must ensure field lines, pressure relief valves, and pump intakes are clear prior to engaging remote actuation. Physical field safety overrides and thermal circuit breakers must remain installed on all pump hardware.

3. Decision-Support Recommendations

Irrigation recommendations generated by the Random Forest classifier and evapotranspiration thresholds depend on the calibration and placement of connected soil probes and third-party meteorological feeds. Operators should verify anomalous probe readings before executing extended watering cycles.

4. Operator Account Security

Each operator is responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their login credentials. Every manual pump toggle and model evaluation is recorded in the Irrigation Actuation Audit Log with the authenticated operator identifier and timestamp.

5. Service Availability

When remote database or weather feeds are unreachable, the platform operates using local embedded storage and cached telemetry. Commands affecting physical pump state require active backend confirmation before status changes are committed.
